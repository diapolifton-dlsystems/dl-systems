What Shapes My Work

Good software starts with understanding the people, process and responsibility behind the project.

1

Problem Solving

I first look at what is not working, who is affected and what a useful outcome should look like before deciding how to build the solution.

2

Systems Thinking

I consider how pages, users, information, workflows and business rules connect instead of treating every feature as an isolated task.

3

Practical Delivery

The result must work across devices, communicate clearly and support the people expected to use it after launch.

4

Continuous Improvement

Every project reveals better ways to structure, test, document and deliver software. I carry those lessons into the next build.
