What We Can Discuss

A consultation is not just a casual chat. It is used to understand your business properly so that the right solution can be planned from the beginning.

This helps avoid building the wrong thing, wasting budget, or starting with features that do not support the business.

Business workflow How your business currently handles customers, bookings, orders, quotes, reports or internal tasks.
Project requirements What the website or system must do, who will use it, and what problem it must solve.
Budget and timeline What is realistic for the project stage, scope, features and expected delivery route.
Best solution direction Whether you need a simple website, business website, booking system, dashboard, automation tool or custom business platform.

Before Booking

To make the consultation useful, prepare a short explanation of what you want to build or what problem your business is currently facing.

Your business type Explain what your business does, who your customers are and what services or products you offer.
Your current process Describe how you currently handle bookings, quotes, enquiries, sales, reports or customer communication.
Your main problem Identify what feels slow, manual, messy, unprofessional or difficult to manage.
Your ideal outcome Think about what should improve: speed, visibility, customer experience, admin control or growth.
