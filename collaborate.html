Collaboration Should Make the Project Stronger

The purpose of working together is not simply to add more people to a project. A useful collaboration brings the right skills, clearer decisions and greater delivery capacity to the work.

DL Systems is open to projects where responsibilities can be defined, communication remains direct and everyone involved is working towards a result that can be used, tested and improved.

How Collaboration Moves Forward

A strong working relationship begins with clear information, realistic expectations and agreed responsibilities.

1

Discuss

The project, existing team, current challenges, expected result, timeline and available resources are discussed.

2

Define

The responsibilities, scope, deliverables, communication route and technical boundaries are agreed.

3

Develop

The work is completed through organised implementation, version control, testing and regular communication.

4

Review and Deliver

The agreed functionality is reviewed before handover, deployment or the next development phase.

The Best Collaborations Begin With Honest Expectations

The size of the project matters less than the quality of the working relationship. A focused project with clear decisions can be more successful than a large project with uncertain ownership and constantly changing requirements.

DL Systems works best with people who are prepared to communicate, make decisions, provide required information and respect the development process.

A good fit Projects with a clear problem, responsible decision-maker, realistic timeline and defined outcome.
Useful information to prepare The current problem, intended users, required features, available content, timeline and expected budget.
Projects requiring discussion first Large platforms, revenue-share arrangements, unfinished third-party code and projects with changing or undocumented requirements.
Employment and contract opportunities Software-development, systems-development and technical-delivery roles can be discussed through the same contact process.
